01 Each team's baseline

Each team is compared with its own history.

Its baseline is what that team usually does, taken from its previous 12 weeks. Every current figure is read against it. No rankings between teams.

↻Tap a card to read what it says
02 Data reliability

Before giving a figure, it checks the data.

Ten checks on the export. A figure that depends on badly recorded data is shown neither as good nor as bad. It is shown as “Check the data”.

    04 Automatic tracking

    And it measures itself with every data load.

    It takes the baseline from before, allows two weeks to settle and compares with the other teams. If every team improves at the same time, the initiative gets no credit for it.
